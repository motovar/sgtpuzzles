Slide

Slide the blocks to let the key block out

A sliding-block puzzle in the style of "Klotski": rectangular blocks of different sizes fill a box. Slide them around so that the large highlighted block can reach the exit on the edge of the box.

This puzzle comes from the "unfinished" folder of Simon Tatham's collection.

Slide controls

Drag a block in the direction you want it to move. A block can only slide into empty space and can be dragged several squares in one go. With a keyboard, move the cursor to a block, press Enter to pick it up, move it with the arrow keys and press Enter again to put it down.

The status bar shows the number of moves you have made and, when known, the minimum number of moves.

Slide parameters

Width, Height
Size of the box.
Solution length limit
Upper limit on the length of the shortest solution of generated puzzles; a negative value means no limit.

Status

Fully playable, with Solve. The 7x6 presets generate quickly; the 8x6 preset can take several seconds.
