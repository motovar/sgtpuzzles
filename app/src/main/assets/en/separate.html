Separate

Partition the grid into regions containing one of each letter

The grid is filled with letters. There are k different letters and each occurs the same number of times. Divide the grid into connected regions of k squares so that every region contains exactly one of each letter.

This puzzle is based on a Nikoli puzzle ("Block Puzzle") and comes from the "unfinished" folder of Simon Tatham's collection, where only the generator existed. The playable interface was written for this app.

Separate controls

Drag your finger from one square across neighbouring squares to join them into a region: a thick bar is drawn between joined squares. If the first step of a drag starts on two squares that are already joined, the drag removes the joins instead (shown in orange). Dragging back over the squares you just crossed shortens the path. Long-press a square to detach it from all its neighbours.

A region with the correct size and one of each letter is drawn in green; a region that has a repeated letter or is too large is drawn in red.

With a keyboard, move the cursor with the arrow keys, press Enter to start a drag, move to extend it and press Enter again to apply it. Space detaches the square under the cursor.

Separate parameters

Width, Height
Size of the grid in squares.
Letters (region size)
The number of different letters k (2 to 12), which is also the size of every region. It must divide the number of squares.

Status

Fully playable, with Solve and a logical next step. The generator is the slow part: the presets (up to 6x6 with 6 letters) take well under a second, but 7x7 with 7 letters and larger boards can take a long time or may not finish.
